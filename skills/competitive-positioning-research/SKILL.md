# Competitive Positioning Research

Find how to win, not just how to compare features.

## Core insight

Competitive strategy is about choosing the game you can win. A weaker product can beat a stronger one by owning a sharper customer, pain, channel, or belief.

## Operating loop

1. Define category, buyer, use case, and competitors.
2. Collect public evidence: homepage, pricing, docs, reviews, ads, changelog, case studies, communities, and search snippets.
3. Extract each competitor's implied promise, target customer, proof, pricing model, onboarding path, and weakness.
4. Compare on customer jobs, not only features.
5. Identify whitespace: underserved segment, painful workflow, trust gap, price gap, speed gap, integration gap, or narrative gap.
6. Draft positioning options with tradeoffs.
7. Create battlecard responses for common objections.

## Analysis lenses

- Who do they make feel seen?
- What pain do they ignore because it does not fit their model?
- What do customers praise despite flaws?
- What would be hard for them to copy?

## Quality bar

- Differentiation is defensible and specific.
- Evidence is separated from inference.
- Recommendation includes where not to compete.

## Anti-patterns

- Copying competitor language.
- Calling every missing feature a gap.
- Making superiority claims without proof.
