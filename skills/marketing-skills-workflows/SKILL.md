# Marketing Skills Workflows

## Overview

This skill enables Claude to assist with comprehensive marketing workflows across:
- SEO
- content strategy
- audience positioning
- funnel optimization
- landing page analysis
- growth marketing
- conversion-focused messaging
- marketing audits

The workflow focuses on building marketing systems that are:
- data-informed
- audience-aware
- conversion-focused
- scalable

- discoverability
- messaging clarity
- conversion rates
- organic traffic

# Core Marketing Principles

## 1. Understand the Audience First

Good marketing begins with understanding:
- audience pain points
- motivations
- objections
- desired outcomes

Claude should identify:
- what users actually care about
- what creates friction
- what builds trust

Avoid:
- generic messaging
- vague positioning
- feature-heavy communication without context

Strong marketing focuses on user outcomes, not just product features.

---

## 2. Clarity Beats Cleverness

Messaging should remain:
- simple
- direct
- understandable
- actionable

Good marketing copy should immediately communicate:
- what the product does
- who it is for
- why it matters

Avoid:
- jargon-heavy copy
- vague slogans
- unclear value propositions

Clear messaging improves:
- conversions
- retention
- trust

---

## 3. SEO & Marketing Should Work Together

SEO should support:
- discoverability
- content authority
- audience acquisition

Marketing should support:
- conversion
- retention
- positioning

Claude should align:
- keyword targeting
- audience messaging
- landing page structure
- content hierarchy

Strong marketing systems integrate both SEO and conversion strategy.

---

## 4. Optimize the Funnel, Not Just Traffic

Traffic alone is not enough.

Claude should evaluate:
- landing page quality
- CTA clarity
- onboarding friction
- trust signals
- content flow

Good funnels improve:
- activation
- engagement
- conversion rates

Avoid:
- driving traffic into weak experiences
- unclear onboarding paths
- conversion dead ends

---

## 5. Build Trust Through Structure

Trust improves conversions significantly.

Important trust elements:
- clean design
- social proof
- clear explanations
- transparent messaging
- strong UX structure

Claude should optimize for:
- clarity
- confidence
- credibility

# Workflow

## 1. Analyze Audience & Positioning

Start by identifying:
- target audience
- core problems
- desired outcomes
- competitor positioning

Evaluate:
- whether messaging matches audience expectations
- whether the product positioning feels differentiated
- whether value propositions remain clear

---

## 2. Audit SEO Structure

Review:
- title tags
- metadata
- heading hierarchy
- keyword targeting
- internal linking
- technical SEO structure

Identify:
- missing optimization opportunities
- weak search intent alignment
- low-quality content structure

Ensure:
- SEO improves discoverability without hurting readability

---

## 3. Review Landing Page Experience

Analyze:
- hero section clarity
- CTA visibility
- section hierarchy
- readability
- trust-building elements

Check for:
- visual clutter
- unclear value propositions
- weak conversion flow
- poor mobile responsiveness

---

## 4. Improve Messaging

Refine:
- headlines
- subheadings
- CTAs
- feature explanations
- onboarding copy

Focus on:
- benefits over features
- audience language
- emotional clarity
- trust

Avoid:
- vague marketing jargon
- overly technical explanations
- hype-heavy messaging

---

## 5. Evaluate Conversion Funnel

Review:
- acquisition flow
- onboarding friction
- signup experience
- CTA placement
- drop-off points

Identify:
- unnecessary complexity
- weak user guidance
- unclear next steps

Optimize:
- conversion clarity
- onboarding simplicity
- user confidence

---

## 6. Improve Content Strategy

Plan:
- blog structure
- content clusters
- SEO support content
- internal linking systems

Good content strategy improves:
- topical authority
- organic growth
- discoverability

Prioritize:
- useful content
- audience relevance
- long-term search value

---

## 7. Final Validation

Before publishing validate:
- messaging clarity
- SEO quality
- conversion flow
- mobile responsiveness
- readability
- trust signals

Ensure:
- marketing feels intentional
- SEO supports usability
- the funnel remains easy to understand
