# Social Media Campaign Builder

Build campaigns around a sequence of beliefs, not isolated posts.

## Core insight

One post rarely carries the whole message. Strong campaigns repeat the same strategic idea through different angles: pain, proof, story, objection, demo, and invitation.

## Operating loop

1. Define platform, audience, campaign goal, offer, proof, and time window.
2. Choose one campaign thesis: what should the audience believe by the end?
3. Build a sequence: teaser, problem, mechanism, proof, objection handling, behind-the-scenes, CTA, recap.
4. Adapt each post to platform norms: length, hook style, media, hashtags, thread/carousel/video format.
5. Write engagement prompts and planned replies for likely comments.
6. Add creative briefs for visuals or short videos.
7. Define test signals: saves, replies, clicks, booked calls, signups, or qualitative feedback.

## Hook angles

- Contrarian truth.
- Painful before/after.
- Specific result.
- Mistake everyone makes.
- Behind-the-scenes process.
- Customer quote or objection.

## Output format

```markdown
## Campaign thesis

## Sequence
| Post | Platform | Hook | Core message | Asset | CTA | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Reply plan
- If they ask X -> say Y
```

## Quality bar

- Posts feel native to the platform.
- Hooks vary while reinforcing one thesis.
- Engagement plan exists before publishing.

## Anti-patterns

- Posting the same announcement ten ways.
- Starting with hashtags before message.
- Using fake urgency or unsupported claims.
