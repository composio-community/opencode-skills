# User Research Synthesis

Turn raw user evidence into product decisions without flattening nuance.

## Core insight

Research synthesis is not summarization. The job is to separate observations, interpretations, and decisions so the team can trust the path from evidence to recommendation.

## Operating loop

1. Inventory sources by type, participant segment, date, and research question.
2. Extract atomic observations: one user, one behavior, one quote, one context.
3. Tag observations by job, trigger, pain, workaround, desired outcome, objection, and language used.
4. Cluster themes only after extraction; do not impose themes too early.
5. Score themes by frequency, intensity, strategic relevance, and confidence.
6. Convert themes into opportunities, risks, and decisions.
7. Preserve counterexamples and minority insights when they reveal high-value segments.

## Synthesis rules

- Frequency tells you how common something is; intensity tells you how painful it is.
- A workaround is stronger evidence than a stated preference.
- Repeated customer language should feed product copy.
- A segment-specific insight should not be generalized to all users.

## Output format

```markdown
## Research Question

## Evidence Base
- Sources:
- Segments:

## Themes
1. Theme

```

## Quality bar

- Every insight points back to evidence.
- Quotes and interpretation are clearly separated.
- Recommendations include confidence level.

## Anti-patterns

- Inventing personas from small samples.
- Averaging away strong pain from a valuable niche.
- Turning every complaint into a roadmap item.
